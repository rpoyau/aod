\subsection{Consequence 6: Coupling, chirality, polyrotor phases, and torsional tension}
\begin{consequence}\label{cons:c6}
Coupled readouts can carry persistent chirality and a finite family of rotor-phase reports. Unresolved phase disagreement on such a family is reported as torsional tension and its corresponding torsional loss. The same disagreement admits a native torsional residual witness on the declared boundary current histories.
\end{consequence}
\paragraph{Depends on.} \Cref{def:observer-coupling,def:chirality,def:polyrotor,def:torsional,def:drift,def:torsional-operator,def:loss-channel}.
\paragraph{Local substructure.} Observer-family coupling; chirality; polyrotor phase family; torsional tension; torsional loss; torsional residual.
\paragraph{Derivation sketch.} From the declared coupling relation and the declared phase-family / torsion bookkeeping on the same active-cut family. When phase disagreement persists, the chirality-resolved boundary histories fail to cancel after alignment, giving a nonzero torsional residual and a positive loss channel on the same readout. The torsional residual is the native boundary witness used below for that disagreement.
