\subsection{Consequence 4: \texorpdfstring{$\alpha\leftrightarrow\omega$}{alpha-omega} backlog, narrowing, and reconciliation}
\begin{consequence}\label{cons:c4}
The local interior tally $\alpha$ and the closure obligation $\omega$ need not agree on a cited cut. Their mismatch is reported as backlog. The successor horizon is narrowed by that backlog and by any declared coupling, and reconciliation services the resulting debt.
\end{consequence}
\paragraph{Depends on.} \Cref{def:present-identity,choice:tau-readout,def:successor-horizon,def:alpha-omega,def:backlog-reconciliation,def:observer-coupling,def:mt,def:loss-channel}.
\paragraph{Local substructure.} $\alpha$ tally; $\omega$ obligation; backlog; coupled successor narrowing; reconciliation; admissibility-loss channel.
\paragraph{Derivation sketch.} The present identity is evaluated on the cited cut. An unresolved return obligation on that same cut family is carried as backlog. Declared coupling and backlog policies reduce the admissible successor family, and the loss channel records the same reduction through $m_t$ and $R_{\mathrm{loss}}$. The Markov Kernel remains the declared update rule on the cited state; backlog acts through realized clocking and admissibility on the same readout family.
