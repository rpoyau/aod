\subsection{Consequence 6: Coupling, chirality, and recursive phase debt service}
\begin{consequence}\label{cons:c6}
Coupled readouts can carry persistent chirality and a finite polyrotor phase family. Persistent unresolved phase disagreement on such a family is recorded as torsional tension and torsional loss. The same disagreement undergoes recursive phase debt service and admits a torsional residual witness on the declared chirality-resolved boundary-current histories; on the same readout family it is accompanied by a positive loss channel.
\end{consequence}
\paragraph{Depends on.} \Cref{def:observer-coupling,def:chirality,def:polyrotor,def:torsional,def:recursive-phase-debt-service,def:torsional-residual-witness,def:loss-channel}.
\paragraph{Local substructure.} Observer-family coupling; chirality; polyrotor phase family; torsional tension; torsional loss; recursive phase debt service; torsional residual witness.
\paragraph{Derivation sketch.} From the declared coupling relation and the declared phase-family / torsion bookkeeping on the same declared cut family. Persistent phase disagreement yields a nonzero torsional residual witness and a positive loss channel on the same chirality-resolved boundary histories. This consequence fixes the witness level only; the explicit operator realization is recorded later.
