\section{Shell paths}\label{sec:shell-paths}

These examples state field, region, shell, and boundary structures directly. Boundary accounting records current and closure across those structures.

\subsection{Hydrogen-facing shared cage}\label{subsec:hydrogen-facing-shared-cage}
The hydrogen-facing shared cage may be written
\[
0\mid-::Z:A:0:6\langle 1:1:0::6\rangle.
\]
Named:
\[
0\mid-::Z:A:0:\mathrm{hexon}\langle \mathrm{Tritrio}::\mathrm{Tetratrio}::\mathrm{hexon}\rangle.
\]
For the filled hexon field, write
\[
0\mid-::Z:A:0:6\langle 3:3:0::6\rangle.
\]
Named:
\[
0\mid-::Z:A:0:\mathrm{hexon}\langle \mathrm{Tritrio}^{3}::\mathrm{Tetratrio}^{3}::\mathrm{hexon}\rangle.
\]
The compact forms are used in Section~\ref{subsec:shared-cage-compression} and Section~\ref{subsec:filled-shared-cageons}.

A stable shell is retained monon-cycle closure on the declared window. A jump changes the retained shell path. Excess disturbance sheds. Shed disturbance may move outward across a shell boundary, reclose through another outer-shell path, or enter a decay path.

\paragraph{Shell pressure difference.}
Shell energy readings begin as shell-pressure differences. For shell paths \(S_{L_1}\) and \(S_{L_2}\), write
\[
\Delta P^D_{\mathrm{shell}}(L_1\to L_2;\omega)=
P^D_{S_{L_2}}(\omega)-P^D_{S_{L_1}}(\omega).
\]
Measured comparisons are downstream calibration studies.

\subsection{Atomic shell-stack pattern}\label{subsec:atomic-shell-stack-pattern}
The same pattern iterates upward through shared cages and filled cages. The active trion-bearing contribution is supplied by \(3:3\), and the fuller tetrad-seating contribution is supplied by \(3:4\). In the compact basis
\[
\mathcal B_{\mathrm{curl}}=(3:3,\ 3:4,\ 1:2),
\]
the filled shared cages are
\[
3:3:0::6,
\qquad
4:4:0::8,
\qquad
5:5:0::10.
\]
Named:
\[
\mathrm{Tritrio}^{3}::\mathrm{Tetratrio}^{3}::\mathrm{hexon}
\to
\mathrm{Tritrio}^{4}::\mathrm{Tetratrio}^{4}::\mathrm{octon}
\to
\mathrm{Tritrio}^{5}::\mathrm{Tetratrio}^{5}::\mathrm{decon}.
\]
Confinement patterns are fixed in Section~\ref{subsec:cageon-confinement-patterns}.

\paragraph{Dimonon shell-path pressure probe.}
For the Dimonon shell path \(1:2:L\), use
\[
\mathcal P^{(0)}_{1:2:L}
=
\frac{2^L+4}{2^6+4}\frac{L!}{6!},
\qquad
L\in\{6,8,10,\ldots\}.
\]
Interpretation:
\[
1:2:6=\mathrm{Dimonhexon},
\qquad
1:2:8=\mathrm{Dimonocton},
\qquad
1:2:10=\mathrm{Dimondecon}.
\]
This probe preserves downstream shell/rest prediction checks without returning to the old rest-rate mechanism.
