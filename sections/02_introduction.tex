\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Relational units and notation}
The note records count/duration quantities in \(\mathrm{bip}_0\), reciprocal-rate quantities in \(\mathrm{biz}_0\), and ternary readouts in trit unless another working cut level is explicitly declared. A cut declares an inside, a boundary, and an outside on the declared Region and boundary. Other local terms are defined only where they first enter the derivation. The level-relative status of \(\mathrm{bip}_0/\mathrm{biz}_0\) is fixed in Appendix~\ref{app:level-relative-bip-biz}.

\subsection{Temporal localization on declared cuts}
Temporal order is read on successive declared cuts. The predecessor trace records what has been cut and carried on a Memory path. The present is the declared cut together with the identity evaluated on its boundary. The successor horizon records what could be cut next under the current Memory State, the Markov Kernel, and the declared local constraints.

\subsection{Cut localization}
On a declared Region $R\subseteq V$ with boundary $\partial R$, the cut localizes the inherited continuity relation into an interior sum and a boundary sum. A declared readout assigns ternary symbols $\tau_t\in\{-1,0,+1\}$ on declared frames and windows in \(\mathrm{bip}_0/\mathrm{biz}_0\) notation unless another working cut level is declared.
