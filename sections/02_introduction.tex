\subsection{Temporal localization (AOD reading)}
On a declared readout family, the predecessor trace records what has been cut and carried along a Memory path. The present is the active cut together with the present identity on that cut. The successor horizon records what could be cut next under the current Memory State, the Markov Kernel, and the declared local constraints. In this sense, temporal order is read across successive active cuts as the ordered relation between what has been cut, the boundary on which the identity is read now, and what could be cut next.
