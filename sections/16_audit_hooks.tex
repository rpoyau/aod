\section{Audit hooks}\label{sec:audit-hooks}

Audit hooks preserve structural checks without creating additional ontology.

\paragraph{Self-similarity audit.}
Define the local motif
\[
B(x)=(x_-,\mu,x_+).
\]
Apply \(x\mapsto (x\succ x=x)\). The audit passes when every retained occurrence at every finite depth has branch/hinge/branch incidence.

\paragraph{Field-as-monon audit.}
Given a complete observer-field \(\mathcal F\) with boundary \(\partial\mathcal F\), bind it as
\[
\mathrm{monon}_{\mathcal F}=\operatorname{cycle}_{\partial\mathcal F}(x\succ x=x).
\]
The audit passes when external boundary accounting preserves the same in/out/pending returned-current relation after the field is bound as one monon cycle.

\paragraph{Tesseract audit.}
A resolved duon has four active dion positions and one monon hinge. Partial unspooling of the four active positions gives \(\{0,1\}^4\). Hamming-1 adjacency gives \(\Qfour\). The audit passes when the finite chart has the \(\Qfour\) support and the monon hinge remains a ternary fibre.


\paragraph{Four-edge \(x\) audit.}
For each retained \(x\)-vertex \(\epsilon\in\{0,1\}^4\), the four one-coordinate updates \(\epsilon\leftrightarrow\epsilon\oplus e_i\), \(i=1,2,3,4\), are present. The audit passes when \(\deg_{\Qfour}(\epsilon)=4\) and the monon hinge remains a ternary fibre over the four-edge support.

\paragraph{Even address audit.}
The fractal address is four-nary even. Retained closure addresses begin
\[
\mathtt{00},\ \mathtt{02},\ \mathtt{10},\ \mathtt{12},\ \mathtt{20},\ \mathtt{22},\ \mathtt{30},\ \mathtt{32},\ \mathtt{100},\ldots .
\]

\paragraph{Cage integrity audit.}
A complete cage identity follows retained cage/shell topology. A partial boundary cut through a cage is a sample through the cage structure.

\paragraph{PADAR audit.}
Each returned-current channel has
\[
\ADAR_e=(A_e,D_e,R^{\mathrm{asym}}_e).
\]
The field dynamics is
\[
\PADAR_{\mathcal F}(\omega)=\{\ADAR_e(\omega)\}_{e\in\mathcal F(\partial\mathcal F,\omega)}.
\]
The audit passes when PADAR is repeated field dynamics over returned-current channels rather than a new topology.

\paragraph{Field-count / cageon split audit.}
Fixed-scope field-count calculations use
\[
\mathcal B\to \mathcal F_{\mathcal B}\to n_F(\mathcal B)\to \ADAR_{i\leftarrow j}\to K_{\partial F}(\mathcal B)\to \PADAR.
\]
Cageon confinement calculations use
\[
\mathcal C_{2m}\to P_{\mathcal C}^{\PADAR}\to C_{\mathcal C}^{\mathrm{close}}\to X_{\mathcal C}\to
\text{reclosure / exoshedding / endoshedding / transmutation routing}.
\]
The audit passes when field-count scaling and cageon confinement remain distinct calculations on the same declared boundary/window.

\paragraph{Wave-handoff audit.}
A declared wave is tracked as motif-carried distinction-information:
\[
Z_f=(\mathfrak m_f,I^c_f,B^e_f)
\to
Z_{f+1}=(\mathfrak m_{f+1},I^c_{f+1},B^e_{f+1}).
\]
Motif compatibility carries coherent handoff. Changed motif compatibility routes excess burden into shedding, reclosure, pending return, or entropic duon current.

\paragraph{Flux/current audit.}
A slice flux is one cut-frame boundary crossing:
\[
\Phi^D_{\partial R}(t).
\]
A window current is the retained many-frame continuation:
\[
J^D_{\partial R}(\omega)
=
\sum_{t\in\omega}\Phi^D_{\partial R}(t).
\]
