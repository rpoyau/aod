\begin{abstract}
Alpha\(\langle\rangle\)Omega Dynamics (A\(\Omega\)D) is an addendum to
Axiomatic-Fundamentalism Calculus (AFC)~\cite{afc}. It traces how the AFC
axiom
\[
\mathcal N\equiv\infty
\]
leads to the cut-running form
\[
\mathcal N\to\mathcal N\to\mathcal N\equiv\mathcal N,
\]
and how that form leads to the hidden temporal dynamics of Stokes.
\end{abstract}
