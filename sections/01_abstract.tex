\begin{abstract}
Proceeding from AFC Axiom~1 and the imported AFC Stokes identity on declared Regions, this note develops Alpha$\leftrightarrow$Omega Dynamics (AOD) as a temporal reading of the same relational continuity on declared cuts, frames, and windows. The imported identity is symmetric at the relational level. Localized on successive active cuts, that same identity is read directionally: the local tally and the closure obligation need not coincide on a given cut, and their mismatch appears as backlog, reconciliation, crowning, return, enclosure, chirality, cadence, and torsional tension. On inherited support, read on the canonical Q4 chart, the note develops the native operator layer for this cut-to-cut unspooling, including admissibility/loss in trit/put, wavelet transport, push/pull and effective-charge bookkeeping, $B^*$-ladder and histogram evaluation with Jensen discipline, torsional residual, export/radiation typing, and collision/rendering rules. In this sense, AOD continues AFC by reading the same imported Stokes identity temporally across successive cuts.
\end{abstract}
