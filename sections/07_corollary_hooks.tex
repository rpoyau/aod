\subsection{Falsification hooks}
\begin{hook}[H1: identity symmetry / internal-cancellation audit]
\label{hook:h1}
A reported cut fails if the AFC identity does not exhibit the expected relational symmetry or internal-cancellation balance under the declared Region/boundary bookkeeping.
\end{hook}

\begin{hook}[H2: isotropic-ignorance / anisotropic-knowledge audit]
\label{hook:h2}
A run fails if anisotropy claims are made without an explicit local knowledge-profile declaration or if isotropic invariance is claimed where the declared refinement breaks it.
\end{hook}

\begin{hook}[H3: topology / two-cycle / four-cycle audit]
\label{hook:h3}
A topology report fails if the canonical Q4 chart or the two-cycle/four-cycle witness structure is not supported by the declared cut trace family.
\end{hook}

\begin{hook}[H4: backlog / narrowing / reconciliation audit]
\label{hook:h4}
A backlog or reconciliation claim fails if successor narrowing, backlog, and reconciliation are not reconstructed from the same cited cut family and policy set.
\end{hook}

\begin{hook}[H5: wavelet / saturation / chained-trit audit]
\label{hook:h5}
A wavelet-passage claim fails if the AO field, wavelet passage, saturation, and loss ledgers do not support the chained trit carriage claim on the cited Memory path.
\end{hook}

\begin{hook}[H6: push/pull / recursive phase debt service / torsional residual witness audit]
\label{hook:h6}
A push/pull or recursive phase debt service claim fails if the chirality-resolved boundary totals, effective-charge proxy, phase-lag witness, torsional residual witness, and positive loss channel are not jointly evidenced on the same readout family.
\end{hook}

\begin{hook}[H7: cadence / dominant residual-duration class / Jensen audit]
\label{hook:h7}
A cadence or dominant residual-duration class claim fails if the declared cadence extractor and dominant residual-duration estimator do not reproduce the cited cadence spectrum, $B^*$ support mass, and the required Jensen-discipline ordering on the same readout family.
\end{hook}


\begin{hook}[H8: confinement / transmutation audit]
\label{hook:h8}
A confinement or transmutation claim fails unless the cited window certifies the shell predicate, shell-signature class, declared $B^*$-dominance, declared proximity rule, and the corresponding failure threshold on the same motif-conditioned ledger.
\end{hook}
