\subsection{Example E1: Active cut, present identity, and symmetry/asymmetry}
\paragraph{Depends on.} \Cref{def:active-cut,def:present-identity,choice:isotropic-ignorance,choice:anisotropic-knowledge,def:loss-channel,cons:c1,hook:h1,hook:h2}.
\paragraph{Local readout.} A declared active cut $c_t$ with one frame $f_t$ on a finite Region $R_t$, together with its boundary ledger and a declared knowledge profile.
\paragraph{Native output.} The present identity on the active cut is
\[
\sum_{i\in R_t}\mathrm{div}_t(i)=\sum_{i\in R_t,\,j\notin R_t}\phi^{(t)}_{ij}.
\]
For a compact native witness, let $\ell_2:=\log_3\!\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)$ and report the same active-cut family under two declared knowledge profiles:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llllll@{}}
\toprule
Row & profile & $T_{\mathrm{eval}}$ [bip] & $N_m=(n_1,n_2,n_3)$ & $R_{\mathrm{loss}}$ [trit/bip] & $R_{\mathrm{loss}}^{(\mathrm{biz})}$ [biz] \\
\midrule
E1.A & isotropic ignorance & $8$ & $(0,2,6)$ & $\ell_2/4$ & $\bigl(3^{\ell_2/4}-1\bigr)$ \\
E1.B & anisotropic knowledge & $8$ & $(1,2,5)$ & $(1+2\ell_2)/8$ & $\bigl(3^{(1+2\ell_2)/8}-1\bigr)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Here $N_m=(n_1,n_2,n_3)$ records the counts of ticks with admissibility cardinality $m_t=1,2,3$ on the same active-cut family. The second row differs only by the declared local refinement and its induced admissibility restriction. The primitive is the trit/bip channel; the biz column is shown only as a derived internal rate.
\paragraph{Hook/evidence.} \Cref{hook:h1,hook:h2}. The same cut family must support both the identity evaluation and the declared knowledge-profile tag.
