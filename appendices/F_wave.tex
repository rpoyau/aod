\section{Wave presentation and audit}
\label{app:wave}

\subsection{Scope}
\label{app:wave:scope}
This appendix records wave as the SADAR-valued ordered-window presentation
of a retained A\(\Omega\) cycle and gives its audit conditions.

\subsection{Cycle / wave identity}
\label{app:wave:cycle-wave-identity}
A cycle is the retained branch/hinge/branch package. For a retained cycle \(C\) on a declared boundary/window \(B=(\partial_TF,\omega)\), its wave presentation is
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{wave}_B(C)
=
\{(t,\SADARop_{B|t}(C)):t\in\omega\}.
\label{app:wave:eq:wave-sadar-presentation}
\end{equation}
Thus the wave is the SADAR-valued ordered-window presentation of \(C\). This
presentation alone declares neither an extractor cadence nor relational
temporal flow. The hinge \(\mu\) implicitly supports chirality through the
handed return presentation
\(A\leftrightarrow_{\mu}\Omega\rightarrow\Omega\leftrightarrow_{\mu}A\rightarrow A\leftrightarrow_{\mu}\Omega\).



\subsection{Fractal growth and successor direction}
\label{app:wave:fractal-growth-temporal-direction}
A branch/hinge/branch package repeats because the declared kernel continues until the current hinge is saturated on the declared support. When the isotropic or anisotropic max-capacity-3 kernel has no admissible continuation through the current hinge, the hinge is closed into the current Alpha/Omega order and the next retained presentation requires a new hinge:
\[
\operatorname{sat}_{\mu}(C;B)=1
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\Pi_{\Omega}(C)=\operatorname{Close}_{B}(\Pi_{\alpha}(C),H_{\mu}(C)).
\]
The successor presentation is then carried by a new hinge \(\mu'\):
\[
C\xrightarrow{\text{closed hinge }\mu}
\operatorname{wave}_{B}(C)
\xrightarrow{\text{new hinge }\mu'}
C'.
\]
Thus successor direction is the retained successor branch after the locked
hinge closes. Every retained cycle, whether seat-retained or free-routed,
has a wave presentation whenever \(\SADARop_{B|t}(C)\) is declared over
\(\omega\).

\subsection{Flux and window current}
\label{app:wave:flux-current}
For a cut-frame \(t\), write
\[
B|_t=(\partial_TF,\{t\}).
\]
The SADAR slice value is
\begin{equation}
\Phi^D_{\partial_T R}(t;B|_t)
=
\sum_{e\in\partial_T R(t)}\SADARop_{B|_t}(C_e).
\label{app:wave:eq:slice-flux}
\end{equation}
The retained many-frame continuation is
\begin{equation}
J^D_{\partial_T R}(\omega;B)
=
\sum_{t\in\omega}\Phi^D_{\partial_T R}(t;B|_t).
\label{app:wave:eq:window-current}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Falsification}
\label{app:wave:falsification}
The wave audit fails on a declared scope if \(\operatorname{wave}_B(C)\) is
used without a retained cycle \(C\), without a declared boundary/window
\(B\), or without the SADAR values \(\SADARop_{B|t}(C)\) over the declared
window. A wave presentation does not substitute for a declared extractor or
an inter-field temporal relation.
