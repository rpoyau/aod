\section{Wave presentation and audit}
\label{app:wave}

\subsection{Scope}
\label{app:wave:scope}
This appendix audits downstream presentations of an already admitted recurrent
wave. Recurrence and semantic admission precede every value transform.

\subsection{Cycle and wave identity}
\label{app:wave:cycle-wave-identity}
Admission and cycle/reclosure counts are \eqref{eq:wave-identity} and
\eqref{eq:cumulative-closure-counts}. The presentation below consumes those
witnesses.

For an already admitted wave \(W_{B,i}\), its SADAR-valued presentation may be
written
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Present}_{B}(W_{B,i})
=
\{(k,\SADARop_{B|k}(W_{B,i})):k\in\omega\}.
\label{app:wave:eq:wave-sadar-presentation}
\end{equation}
This presentation consumes the admitted identity and committed trace; it does
not create either one.

\subsection{Continuation and direction}
\label{app:wave:fractal-growth-temporal-direction}
Use the advance \eqref{eq:crown-advance} and running/standing classification
\ref{eq:native-running-standing}, retaining each contributing identity and orientation.

\subsection{Flux and window current}
\label{app:wave:flux-current}
The slice flux \eqref{eq:slice-flux} and retained window current
\eqref{eq:window-current} are downstream value coordinates of the admitted
wave. Aggregate cancellation preserves both oriented channel histories.

\subsection{Falsification}
\label{app:wave:falsification}
The audit fails if a presentation is used without an admitted recurring wave,
declared boundary/window, committed values, lineage provenance, or the required
closure and reclosure witnesses. Exact Fourier inversion of an unbound word
does not repair any missing witness.
