\paragraph{Closure roles.}
\label{app:closure-role-separation}
The closure operation certifies a completed retained-start return. A compatibility
value records the result of a declared comparison; mutable status records whether
a continuation is pending, seated or shedding at a cut. Retained closure lineage
records the immutable ancestry of completed returns. These have separate types:
changing status or obtaining a compatible scalar value neither creates another
return nor erases its lineage. Equations \eqref{eq:closure-certificate},
\eqref{eq:return-identity} and \eqref{eq:complete-wave-state} fix their interfaces.

\paragraph{Index discipline.}
\label{app:index-discipline}
The resolved update index \(k\), scale \(\ell\), additional hinge dwell \(m\),
recurrence order \(L\), route radius \(r\), and primitive-order marker \((0)\)
have distinct roles. An expression that uses a different local letter declares
that role at its definition. The inherited odd support-count model is
\begin{equation}
\nu_\ell=2\ell+1,\qquad \ell\in\mathbb N_0,\qquad \nu_0=1.
\label{eq:odd-support-index}
\end{equation}
It counts support in that model; it supplies neither closure phase, recurrence
separation nor RCD duration. In particular, its index \(\ell\) does not identify
the atomic update count \(k\).

\subsection{Fractal address binder}
\label{app:fractal-address-bip-biz}

\subsection{Scope}
\label{app:fractal-address:scope}
A retained calculation declares a fractal coordinate when boundary accounting requires an explicit retained location.

\subsection{Setup}
\label{app:fractal-address:setup}
The coordinate is a left-context binder for the retained cut, field, boundary, and window:
\[
\mathbf a\vdash(\mathcal F,\partial\mathcal F,\omega).
\]

\subsection{Statement(s)}
\label{app:fractal-address:statements}
Distinct reclosure counts are recorded in \(\mathrm{bip}\) and positive-denominator cadence ratios in \(\mathrm{biz}\) under the declared fractal coordinate context:
\[
\mathbf a\vdash \mathrm{bip},\mathrm{biz}.
\]
When the coordinate must be displayed, write
\[
\mathrm{bip}_{\mathbf a},\qquad \mathrm{biz}_{\mathbf a}.
\]
At the first finite anchor, for lineages meeting their respective closure and recurrence conditions,
\[
\mathtt{00}_8\vdash \mathrm{monon},\mathrm{bip},\mathrm{biz}.
\]
