Term & Meaning in this note\\
\midrule
Null symmetry & Symmetric unresolved closure under which branch selection is presented.\\
Cut-running & Asymmetric running relation written \(x\succ x=x\).\\
Hinge triad & Branch/hinge/branch support \(\mathsf H_3=(x^-,\mu,x^+)\). \apref{app:cc-hinge-triad}.\\
Fractal tesseract & Self-similar branch/hinge/branch topology of recursive cut-running.\\
Four-edge \(x\) witness & Retained \(x\)-occurrence as a \(\Qfour\) vertex with four Hamming-1 tesseract edges; the monon hinge remains the ternary fibre. Sec.~\ref{sec:fractal-tesseract-jittering}.\\
Fractal address & Four-nary even retained address. Origin anchor \(\mathtt{00}\); retained sequence begins \(\mathtt{00},\mathtt{02},\mathtt{10},\mathtt{12},\ldots\).\\
Fractal coordinate & The left glyph \(a\mid-\), used in forms such as \(0\mid-::Z:A:0:6\). Sec.~\ref{subsec:coord-zero-cageon-grouping}.\\
Field / observer & Retained closure structure whose regions, polychiral shell dressing, and boundaries carry returned duon current.\\
Memorum & The field's predecessor closure, present boundary, and successor horizon.\\
Region & Declared retained part of a field.\\
Boundary & Retained interface of a declared region, shell, or field.\\
Fixed boundary/window & Scope \(\mathcal B=(\partial F,\omega)\) used for field-count and PADAR calculations. Sec.~\ref{sec:field-count-padar-scope}.\\
Retained field family & \(\mathcal F_{\mathcal B}=\{F_1,\ldots,F_{n_F}\}\), the fields retained on fixed scope \(\mathcal B\).\\
Field count & \(n_F(\mathcal B)=|\mathcal F_{\mathcal B}|\), the number of retained field participants on the fixed boundary/window.\\
Poly term & \(K_{\partial F}(\mathcal B)=n_F(\mathcal B)\log n_F(\mathcal B)\), with logarithm base declared by the calculation.\\
Polychiral shell dressing & Field outer dressing; it carries alpha/Omega return orientation, polarization imbalance, return duration, pressure, and PADAR coupling.\\
Curl & Returned closure through a hinge.\\
Monon & Primitive completed cycle of \(x\succ x=x\).\\
Dion & First two-position return curl, \(D_\alpha=(+1,-1)\) or \(D_\Omega=(-1,+1)\).\\
Duon current & Carried hinge-current \(D_i\mu D_j\) through knot support.\\
Duad & Seat-closed state of the same duon current, \(E_2^\pm\).\\
Wavelet group & Carried segment of duon current.\\
Lead--lag disturbance & Carried asymmetric return span of a wavelet group.\\
Shedding & Break of excess carried disturbance from local closure compatibility.\\
Exoshedding & Closure excess crossing the outer cage boundary. Sec.~\ref{subsec:cageon-padar-closure}.\\
Endoshedding & Closure excess retained through an inner reclosure path. Sec.~\ref{subsec:cageon-padar-closure}.\\
Transmutation routing & Closure excess redirected into a different retained cageon or shell path after PADAR closure changes.\\
Tetrion & Bare four-cycle / four-pivot return closure. \apref{app:cc-tetrion-tetron-tetratrion}.\\
Tetron & \(E_4\), the default inner cage with four seats. Sec.~\ref{subsec:cageon-even-forms}.\\
Cageon & Outer even confinement cage \(\mathsf C_{2m}\), \(m\ge3\). Sec.~\ref{subsec:outer-cageons}.\\
Tetratrion & Core family \(3:4=4\times\mathrm{trion}\). \apref{app:cc-active-core-families}.\\
Dimonon & Core family \(1:2=2\times\mathrm{monon}\). \apref{app:cc-active-core-families}.\\
Trio & Core family \(3:1=1\times\mathrm{trion}\). \apref{app:cc-active-core-families}.\\
Ditrion & Core family \(3:2=2\times\mathrm{trion}\). \apref{app:cc-active-core-families}.\\
Tritrion & Core family \(3:3=3\times\mathrm{trion}\). \apref{app:cc-active-core-families}.\\
Dimonhexon & Caged name \(1:2:6\). \apref{app:cc-standalone-caged-names}.\\
Tritriohexon & Caged name \(3:3:6\). \apref{app:cc-standalone-caged-names}.\\
Tetratriohexon & Caged name \(3:4:6\). \apref{app:cc-standalone-caged-names}.\\
Dimonocton & Lifted caged name \(1:2:8\). \apref{app:cc-standalone-caged-names}.\\
Dimondecon & Lifted caged name \(1:2:10\). \apref{app:cc-standalone-caged-names}.\\
Hexon & Outer cageon \(\mathsf C_6\). Sec.~\ref{subsec:outer-cageons}.\\
Octon & Outer cageon \(\mathsf C_8\). Sec.~\ref{subsec:outer-cageons}.\\
Decon & Outer cageon \(\mathsf C_{10}\). Sec.~\ref{subsec:outer-cageons}.\\
Tritrio::Tetratrio::hexon & Shared cage name \(1:1:0::6\). \apref{app:cc-basic-shared-hexon}.\\
RCD & Local name for PADAR's duration coordinate when isolated with asymmetric reflection, \(D_e\leftrightarrow R^{\mathrm{asym}}_e\). Sec.~\ref{sec:rcd-pending-return}.\\
Duonic pressure & Scalar burden of retained or pending duon/trit current.\\
Entropic duon current & Retained duon current carrying unresolved return burden.\\
ADAR & One local returned-current channel: Attention--Duration--Asymmetric Reflection.\\
PADAR & Field dynamics: Poly Attention--Duration--Asymmetric Reflection across regions, polychiral shell dressing, and boundaries.\\
Attention vector & Pressure-weighted relational motion of the field; direction comes from boundary orientation/incidence/chirality and signed lead--lag bias, while magnitude comes from duonic pressure, retained duration, and PADAR burden.\\
Crowning & Selection of the present by the cut, \(\operatorname{Crown}(x)=x^0\). Sec.~\ref{sec:null-cut-branch-hinge}.\\
Alpha advantage & Normalized excess of crowned alpha selections over Omega closures, \(\Delta_\alpha=(N_\alpha-N_\Omega)/N_{\mathrm{tot}}\). \apref{app:stochastic-crowning}.\\

Wave & Markovian or retained-history entropic handoff of distinction-information through a recurring relational motif. In AOD, the local motif is the duon wavelet \(W[D_i\mu D_j]\). \apref{app:wave}.\\
Entropic carrier & Handoff pattern carrying coherent distinction-information and unresolved burden beyond motif compatibility.\\
Wave handoff & Continuation of motif-carried distinction-information from one declared cut-frame to another. \apref{app:wave:statements}.\\
Markovian handoff & Handoff whose next state follows the present carried state. \apref{app:wave:statements:continuity}.\\
Retained-history handoff & Handoff whose next state follows present state together with retained return, delay, reflection, jitter, lead--lag, or pending closure. \apref{app:wave:statements:continuity}.\\
Flux & One declared cut-frame crossing through a boundary. \apref{app:wave:flux-current}.\\
Current & Many flux crossings retained across a declared boundary/window. \apref{app:wave:flux-current}.\\
Support condition & Relation-continuation capacity for handoff. \apref{app:wave:setup}.\\
