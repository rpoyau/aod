\section{Fractal address}
\label{app:fractal-address-bip-biz}

\subsection{Scope}
\label{app:fractal-address:scope}
A retained calculation declares a fractal coordinate when boundary accounting requires an explicit retained location.

\subsection{Setup}
\label{app:fractal-address:setup}
The coordinate is a left-context binder for the retained cut, field, boundary, and window:
\[
\mathbf a\vdash(\mathcal F,\partial\mathcal F,\omega).
\]

\subsection{Statement(s)}
\label{app:fractal-address:statements}
Counts are recorded in \(\mathrm{bip}\) and reciprocal rates in \(\mathrm{biz}\) under the declared fractal coordinate context:
\[
\mathbf a\vdash \mathrm{bip},\mathrm{biz}.
\]
When the coordinate must be displayed, write
\[
\mathrm{bip}_{\mathbf a},\qquad \mathrm{biz}_{\mathbf a}.
\]
At the origin anchor,
\[
\mathtt{00}_8\vdash \mathrm{monon},\mathrm{bip},\mathrm{biz}.
\]
The object \(\mathtt{00}_8\) is the anchor bip. Under the declared
Null-to-anchor incidence chart,
\[
d_H(\mathcal N,\mathtt{00}_8)=1.
\]
This \(1\) is native incidence: one Hamming--1 distinction from Null
potential. Intrinsic elapsed duration is absent from the anchor type. An
elapsed-duration report requires a declared second bip, a bip-to-bip
relation, a return or closure witness, and an extractor.
