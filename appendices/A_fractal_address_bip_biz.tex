\section{Fractal coordinate bip/biz accounting}
\label{app:fractal-address-bip-biz}

A retained calculation declares a fractal coordinate \(\mathbf a\) when boundary accounting requires an explicit retained location. The coordinate is a left-context binder for the retained cut, field, boundary, and window:
\[
\mathbf a\vdash(\mathcal F,\partial\mathcal F,\omega).
\]

Counts are read in \(\mathrm{bip}\) and reciprocal rates in \(\mathrm{biz}\) under the declared fractal coordinate context:
\[
\mathbf a\vdash \mathrm{bip},\mathrm{biz}.
\]
When the coordinate must be displayed, write
\[
\mathrm{bip}_{\mathbf a},\qquad \mathrm{biz}_{\mathbf a}.
\]
At the origin anchor,
\[
\mathtt{00}\vdash \mathrm{monon},\mathrm{bip},\mathrm{biz}.
\]
