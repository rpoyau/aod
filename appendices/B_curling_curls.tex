\subsubsection{Trion / tetrion / tetron / tetratrion / tetrad}
\label{app:cc-tetrion-tetron-tetratrion}
\[
\mathrm{tetrion}=\text{bare four-cycle / four-pivot return closure},
\]
\[
\mathrm{tetron}\ \text{is the }E_4\text{ enclosure of }\secref{subsec:e4-tetron},
\]
\[
\mathrm{tetratrion}=4\times\mathrm{trion}=3:4.
\]
Standalone trion is the threefold core unit. A tetrad is the seated tetron closure. Admission of each named entry resolves its scope, lineage and certificate through \eqref{eq:motif-admission}.

\[
\mathrm{Tritrio}^{a}::\mathrm{Tetratrio}^{b}::\mathrm{Dimon}^{c}::\operatorname{out}(L).
