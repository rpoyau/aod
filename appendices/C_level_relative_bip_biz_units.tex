\section{Level-relative \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{bip}_{\ell}/\mathrm{biz}_{\ell}$}{bip/biz} units}
\label{app:level-relative-bip-biz}

Counts at a declared cut level \(\ell\) are recorded in \(\mathrm{bip}_{\ell}\). Reciprocal rates at the same level are recorded in \(\mathrm{biz}_{\ell}\).

When the reference convention \(\ell=0\) is declared, the corresponding level is written \(\mathrm{monon}_0\), with count unit \(\mathrm{bip}_0\) and reciprocal-rate unit \(\mathrm{biz}_0\).

The cut hierarchy is level-relative. The declared level fixes the row for the declared calculation.
