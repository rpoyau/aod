\section{Level-relative \texorpdfstring{$\mathrm{bip}_{\ell}/\mathrm{biz}_{\ell}$}{bip/biz} units}
\label{app:level-relative-bip-biz}

Counts at a declared cut level \(\ell\) are recorded in \(\mathrm{bip}_{\ell}\). Reciprocal rates at the same level are recorded in \(\mathrm{biz}_{\ell}\). When the manual uses the reference convention \(\ell=0\), it writes \(\mathrm{monon}_0\), \(\mathrm{bip}_0\), and \(\mathrm{biz}_0\). The hierarchy has no bottom cut; \(\mathrm{bip}_0\) is not Planck time.

Any conversion from \(\mathrm{bip}_{\ell}\) or \(\mathrm{biz}_{\ell}\) into seconds, hertz, mass-rate anchors, distance reports, or other SI-report quantities is a declared calibration step downstream of the exact row.
