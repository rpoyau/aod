\section{Wave}
\label{app:wave}

\subsection{Scope}
\label{app:wave:scope}
This App records wave as an AOD consequence of cut-running, duon current, lead--lag burden, shedding, entropic duon current, and PADAR.

\subsection{Setup}
\label{app:wave:setup}
Fix a declared field \(\mathcal F\), retained boundary \(\partial F\), window \(\omega\), and cut-frame index \(f\). Let
\[
D_i,D_j\in\{D_\alpha,D_\Omega\},
\qquad
\mu\in\{-1,0,+1\}.
\]
The duration coordinate used below is \(D_e\), the duration component of
\[
\ADAR_e=(A_e,D_e,R_e^{\mathrm{asym}}).
\]
Support is relation-continuation capacity: the declared capacity to receive, retain, modify, and hand off motif form.

\subsection{Statement(s)}
\label{app:wave:statements}

\subsubsection{Duon entropic wavelet}
\label{app:wave:statements:duon-entropic-wavelet}
A wave is an entropic continuity of distinction-information through a recurring motif. In AOD, the local form is the duon entropic wavelet
\begin{equation}
W[D_i\mu D_j].
\label{app:wave:eq:duon-wavelet}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Wave state}
\label{app:wave:statements:state}
A wave state is
\begin{equation}
Z_f=(\mathfrak m_f,I^c_f,B^e_f),
\label{app:wave:eq:state}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathfrak m_f\) is the carried motif, \(I^c_f\) is coherent distinction-information, and \(B^e_f\) is unresolved entropic burden.

\subsubsection{Continuity}
\label{app:wave:statements:continuity}
A Markovian continuity has the form
\begin{equation}
Z_{f+1}=H_f(Z_f).
\label{app:wave:eq:markovian}
\end{equation}
A retained-history continuity has the form
\begin{equation}
Z_{f+1}=H_f(Z_f,\mathcal R_{\le f}),
\label{app:wave:eq:retained-history}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathcal R_{\le f}\) records retained return, residue, lag, reflection, and pending burden.

\subsection{Flux and current}
\label{app:wave:flux-current}
A slice flux is one cut-frame crossing:
\begin{equation}
\Phi^D_{\partial R}(t)
=
\sum_{e\in\partial R(t)}
\epsilon_e C_{3,e}.
\label{app:wave:eq:slice-flux}
\end{equation}
A window current is retained many-frame continuation:
\begin{equation}
J^D_{\partial R}(\omega)
=
\sum_{t\in\omega}
\Phi^D_{\partial R}(t).
\label{app:wave:eq:window-current}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Derivation}
\label{app:wave:derivation}
The AOD wave form follows from
\[
x\mapsto x\succ x=x
\to
\operatorname{monon}(x)
\to
D_i\mu D_j
\to
W[D_i\mu D_j].
\]
Motif-compatible continuity preserves coherent distinction-information. Changed motif compatibility contributes unresolved burden to shedding, reclosure, pending return, or entropic duon current.

The entropic duon-current contribution is
\begin{equation}
J^D_{\mathrm{ent}}(\partial F,\omega)
=
\sum_e
C_{3,e}
\min(D_e,|\omega|_e)
|\Delta^{\chi\mathrm{lag}}_e|.
\label{app:wave:eq:entropic-current}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Falsification}
\label{app:wave:falsification}
This App fails on a declared cut-frame if the motif state cannot be followed through the declared continuity
\[
Z_f=(\mathfrak m_f,I^c_f,B^e_f)
\to
Z_{f+1}=(\mathfrak m_{f+1},I^c_{f+1},B^e_{f+1}),
\]
or if the window current differs from retained many-frame continuation
\[
J^D_{\partial R}(\omega)
=
\sum_{t\in\omega}
\Phi^D_{\partial R}(t).
\]
