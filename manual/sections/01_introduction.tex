\section{Introduction}
\label{man:introduction}

This manual shows how to perform AOD simulations and predictions from cited field rows. Each worked example identifies a retained AO field, declares a working level and window, runs the AFC/AOD chain, fixes an exact row, and only then maps that row into a cited report or benchmark layer. When a measurement-facing output uses SI units, the SI reference is the BIPM SI Brochure and numerical constants or conversion factors are cited from NIST CODATA listings~\cite{bipm_si_brochure_2025,nist_constants_complete}.
