\section{Temporal conversions}

\subsection{Conversion rules}
\label{man:temporal-conversion-rules}

\paragraph{Question.}
How are cited rows carried into converted witness form without changing the authority of the cited ledger?

\paragraph{Readout.}
Cited rows remain authoritative. Conversion is attached only after the cited row has been fixed.

\paragraph{Temporal Conversion Block.}
Duration converts to \textbf{seconds}. Rate converts to \textbf{hertz}. Effective speed is reported only as
\[
\beta=\frac{v}{c},\qquad c=1,\qquad 0\le \beta\le 1.
\]

\paragraph{Converted Witness Table.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lXl@{}}
\toprule
Cited row & Conversion or rendering target & Note \\
\midrule
Loss-channel worked row & bip-to-seconds, hertz, joule, kilogram, MeV/$c^2$ dictionaries & derived temporal-unit conversion \\
$B^*$ ladder row & decimal ladder summaries / attractor plots & non-premise compression \\
Collision / export row & diagrammatic temporal conversion illustrations & diagrammatic rendering \\
Field-property row & temporal-conversion dashboards / converted tables & cited row followed by converted witness \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Hook / Evidence.}
Use the cited row first. Attach converted witnesses afterward only on the same cited ledger.
