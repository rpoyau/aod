\section{AOD simulation workflow}
\label{man:aod-simulation-workflow}

AOD simulations are local field extractions on declared windows. The calculus is invariant across scale; the retained field, working level, temporal calibration, and truncation residual are what change.

\subsection{Operational sequence}
\label{man:workflow-operational-sequence}

A worked simulation proceeds in this order.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item Identify the parent AO field \(\mathcal F\).
  \item Identify the coalesced subfields carried by \(\mathcal F\).
  \item Declare the retained extraction \(\mathcal F_K\subset\mathcal F\).
  \item Declare omitted context \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{omit}}\).
  \item Declare the first refinement \(\mathcal F_{K+1}\) when it is used.
  \item Declare the working cut level \(\ell\) and the units \(\mathrm{bip}_{\ell}/\mathrm{biz}_{\ell}\).
  \item Declare the window \(\omega\) and the TTL or validity horizon.
  \item Run the AOD operator chain on the retained field.
  \item Produce the exact row.
  \item State the truncation residual.
  \item Apply the exact-to-report map.
  \item Compare with the cited benchmark.
  \item State the falsification condition.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Retention and refinement block}
\label{man:workflow-retention-block}

Every worked example contains a retention/refinement block.
\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Required retention/refinement declarations for a worked AOD simulation.}
\label{tab:retention-refinement-template}
\begin{threeparttable}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
Item & Declaration \\
\midrule
Parent field & \(\mathcal F\) \\
Retained extraction & \(\mathcal F_K\subset\mathcal F\) \\
Omitted context & declared list or class \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{omit}}\) \\
First refinement & \(\mathcal F_{K+1}\), when declared \\
Validity window & \(\omega\) \\
Residual witness & \(\widehat\epsilon_{K\to K+1}(\omega)\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\ManualTableNote{A qualitative residual is only a placeholder until the refinement row is computed.}
\end{threeparttable}
\end{table}

The refinement residual for a row functional \(Q\) is
\[
\widehat\epsilon_{K\to K+1}(\omega)
=
\left|
Q(\mathcal F_{K+1},\omega)-Q(\mathcal F_K,\omega)
\right|.
\]

\subsection{Prototype retained extraction}
\label{man:workflow-mercury-prototype}

For the Mercury prototype, the cited parent field is the solar \(n\)-soma \(\mathcal N_{\mathrm{solar}}(t_0)\). The retained extraction is the Sun--Mercury disoma
\[
\mathcal D_{\odot,\mathrm{mercury}}\subset\mathcal N_{\mathrm{solar}}.
\]
The working level is declared as \(\ell=0\), with exact rows recorded in \(\mathrm{bip}_0\) and \(\mathrm{biz}_0\). Earth--Moon may be retained as nested cadence context; Jupiter is the first large outer-field refinement. Mercury remains blocked until the exact map \(\Phi_{\varpi}\) is written.
