\subsection{Neuronal-ring benchmark acquisition}\label{manual:registry:neuronal-benchmark-acquisition}
This registry subsection supplies a measured acquisition declaration for a field-ring benchmark record. For a biological benchmark acquisition, declare
\[
\mathcal S_{\mathrm{neuro}}
=
(\mathcal M,\Delta t,\mathcal B_{\mathrm{spike}},\kappa_k,\Theta_{\mathrm{stim}},\Omega_{\mathrm{meas}}).
\]
Here
\[
\mathcal M\in
\{\text{spike counts},\ \text{calcium bins},\ \text{voltage bins},\ \text{LFP bins}\}
\]
is the measurement mode, \(\Delta t\) is the bin width, \(\mathcal B_{\mathrm{spike}}\) is the bin extraction rule, \(\Theta_{\mathrm{stim}}\) is the gate-stimulus declaration, and \(\Omega_{\mathrm{meas}}\) is the measurement window.
The fixture comparator is
\[
O_k=\mathcal S_{\mathrm{neuro}}(k;\Omega_{\mathrm{meas}}).
\]

\aodnoteblock{Artifact}{\(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{neuro}}, T_k^0, C_{ab,k}^{\times}(B), T_k^\times, O_k, \Delta_k^{\mathbb Z}, \delta_{3,k}\). If \(O_k\) is continuous or averaged, write \(O_k=x_k\pm\sigma_k\) and treat the comparison as a measured acquisition value map with a decimal precision record.}
\aodnoteblock{Completion rule}{The neuronal-ring benchmark record is complete when benchmark acquisition is declared before bin comparison, the independent gate-sum value is computed as $T^0_k=S^{(a)}_k+S^{(b)}_k$, and the cross-return row records a declared $C_{ab,k}^{\times}(B)$.}
\aodremark{This subsection is a benchmark-acquisition record. The A\(\Omega\) calculation is the recurrent ring value; the measured acquisition declaration is supplied before comparison.}



\subsection{SPARC benchmark declaration}\label{manual:registry:sparc}
The SPARC five-galaxy fixture is carried as a current field-dynamics square-speed 2D observable-data lane. Derived residual and score outputs, where declared, are diagnostics of the projection/readout. The full protocol and scored records are given in \secref{manual:field-dynamics-applications}.
\[
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{SPARC}}
=
\{G_i,r_{ij},V^{\mathrm{obs}}_{ij},\sigma_{V,ij},V^{\mathrm{gas}}_{ij},V^{\mathrm{disk}}_{ij},V^{\mathrm{bulge}}_{ij},L_{3.6,i},\ldots\}.
\]
The active comparison coordinate is square speed:
\[
U^{\mathrm{obs}}_{ij}=(V^{\mathrm{obs}}_{ij})^2,
\qquad
U_{ij,\AO}=\SADARop^U_{B_i}(C_{i,j}).
\]
For each radial bin, the completed record carries
\[
U_{ij,\AO},\qquad
\Delta U_{ij}=U_{ij,\AO}-U^{\mathrm{obs}}_{ij},\qquad
z^U_{ij}=\Delta U_{ij}/\sigma_{U,ij}.
\]
For each galaxy, the completed record carries
\[
\chi^2_U,\qquad
\overline{\Delta U},\qquad
MAE_U,\qquad
MSE_U,\qquad
MAPE_U.
\]
\aodliteraturenote{SPARC benchmark: SPARC database~\cite{sparc-database}, Lelli et al.~\cite{lelli-sparc-2016}.}

\aodnoteblock{Completion rule}{External-comparison rows record numeric map value, target, residual, percent error, and audit hook. Observable-map fixture rows record map value, target, residual, percent error, citation, fixture status, and audit hook. Benchmark-dataset rows record benchmark data table, A\(\Omega\) calculation row, report coordinate, residual, and audit hook. Cross-return rows record declared \(C^\times_{ab,k}(B)\).}


\aodnoteblock{Registry note}{Decimal precision records accompany manual artifacts that use decimal target/value-map values.}
