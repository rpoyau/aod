\section{Molecular chain-fusion worked row}

The molecular example uses the glycine--alanine peptide seed. The route unit is one water-equivalent shedding row.

\subsection{Declare the components}

The free-molecule formulas are
\[
\mathrm{Gly}=\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{H}_5\mathrm{N}\mathrm{O}_2,
\qquad
\mathrm{Ala}=\mathrm{C}_3\mathrm{H}_7\mathrm{N}\mathrm{O}_2,
\qquad
H_2O=\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}.
\]
In named CHNOPS coordinates,
\[
\mathbf n(\mathrm{Gly})=(2,5,1,2,0,0),
\]
\[
\mathbf n(\mathrm{Ala})=(3,7,1,2,0,0),
\qquad
\mathbf n(H_2O)=(0,2,0,1,0,0).
\]

\subsection{Compute the fusion row}

The declared chain-fusion row is
\[
\boxed{\mathbf n(\mathrm{Gly})+\mathbf n(\mathrm{Ala})-\mathbf n(H_2O)=\mathbf n(\mathrm{Gly\mbox{-}Ala}).}
\]
Coordinate by coordinate:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
 & C&H&N&O&P&S\\
\midrule
\mathrm{Gly} & 2&5&1&2&0&0\\
\mathrm{Ala} & 3&7&1&2&0&0\\
-H_2O & 0&-2&0&-1&0&0\\
\midrule
\mathrm{Gly\mbox{-}Ala} & 5&10&2&3&0&0
\end{array}
\]
Hence the frozen formula is
\[
\mathrm{C}_5\mathrm{H}_{10}\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}_3.
\]
The check formula in the ledger is the same, so
\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{CHNOPS}}=(0,0,0,0,0,0),
\qquad
\sum |\Delta_{\mathrm{CHNOPS}}|=0.
\]
