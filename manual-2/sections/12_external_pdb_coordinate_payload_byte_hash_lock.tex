\begin{array}{rcl}

\]

\subsection{AOD freeze remains upstream}

The external payload remains a downstream target object. It never enters the raw D.E.C. row and never enters the AOD prediction-freeze packet. The upstream detection chain remains\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
