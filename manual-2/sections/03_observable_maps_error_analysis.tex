\section{Observable maps and error analysis}

Observable maps are deliberately late; the observable maps phrase is intentional because this section fixes the downstream reporting layer. Observable mapping is deliberately late. The map is written
\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{obs},\ell}:r_i\mapsto (y_i,\hat y_i,\epsilon_i,s_i),
\]
where $y_i$ is the external coordinate, $\hat y_i$ is the AOD report coordinate, $\epsilon_i$ is the residual/error record, and $s_i$ is the support source.

\subsection{Required error ledger}

Every comparison row must be able to carry the following fields:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
Field & Role \\
\midrule
\texttt{exact\_row\_id} & frozen trace or fixture row being reported \\
\texttt{observable\_name} & declared external coordinate or descriptor \\
\texttt{external\_dataset} & source lane; never a core premise \\
\texttt{external\_value} & value read from the external lane \\
\texttt{aod\_report\_value} & value produced by the frozen row and report map \\
\texttt{absolute\_error} & $|\hat y-y|$ where applicable \\
\texttt{relative\_error} & $|\hat y-y|/|y|$ where applicable \\
\texttt{residual\_class} & zero, capacity-minus-target, formula residual, graph parse status, or other declared class \\
\texttt{failure\_hook} & condition that would demote or reject the row \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\subsection{Non-negotiable ordering}

The order is:
\[
\text{exact row} \prec \text{observable map} \prec \text{external comparison} \prec \text{interpretation}.
\]
A row may be interesting only after comparison, but it must be well-defined before comparison.

\subsection{Failure hooks}

Manual II uses ordinary failure hooks:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item missing boundary declaration;
  \item detector uses an external target before freeze;
  \item nonzero residual silently dropped;
  \item PubChem or RDKit row treated as an AFC premise;
  \item chain formula ledger fails exact CHONPS accounting;
  \item observable map lacks source, support class, or error field.
\end{itemize}
