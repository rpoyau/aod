\section{Protein contact residual worked row}

This row compares one already frozen AOD contact/reclosure packet against one already derived target contact bit.

\subsection{Frozen prediction and target}

The prediction-freeze ledger gives
\[
\widehat O_{1,3}=1.
\]
The target derivation ledger gives
\[
O_{1,3}=1.
\]
The integer residual is therefore
\[
\Delta^{\mathbb Z}_{1,3}
=\widehat O_{1,3}-O_{1,3}
=1-1=0.
\]
The retained contact residual class is
\[
\delta_3=(0,0).
\]

\subsection{Confusion row}

Because the prediction and target are both contacts, the pair is a true positive:
\[
TP=1,
\qquad
FP=0,
\qquad
FN=0,
\qquad
TN=0.
\]
The summary metrics over this declared pair subset are
\[
\mathrm{precision}=1,
\qquad
\mathrm{recall}=1,
\qquad
F_1=1,
\qquad
J=1.
\]
The MCC denominator has no true-negative support in this one-pair subset, so the ledger records it as undefined rather than inventing a value.
