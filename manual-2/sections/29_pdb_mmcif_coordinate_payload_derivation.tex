\section{PDB/mmCIF coordinate-payload derivation gate}

The v40.02r07 milestone is a target-ingest and contact-map derivation gate. It reads committed PDBx/mmCIF-style coordinate payload fixtures and derives target-only coordinate, distance, and contact rows. It does not read AOD prediction packets and it does not compute an expanded AOD-versus-PDB score.

The ordering is:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}X@{}}
\texttt{committed PDBx/mmCIF coordinate payload fixture} \\
\textrightarrow{} \texttt{atom-site extraction} \\
\textrightarrow{} \texttt{residue coordinate table} \\
\textrightarrow{} \texttt{CA distance rows} \\
\textrightarrow{} \texttt{derived target-contact rows}. 
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\subsection{Target-only files}

The v40.02r07 files are:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
File & Role \\
\midrule
\texttt{pdb\_mmcif\_coordinate\_payload\_registry.csv} & committed payload registry, hashes, threshold, scope, and no-score status \\
\texttt{pdb\_mmcif\_atom\_site\_extract.csv} & parsed atom-site rows from the committed PDBx/mmCIF fixture \\
\texttt{pdb\_mmcif\_residue\_coordinate\_table.csv} & residue-level CA/CB coordinate table used for target derivation \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\subsection{Contact derivation rule}

For residues $i<j$, the target distance row uses the CA distance
\[
 d_{ij}=\|\mathrm{CA}_i-\mathrm{CA}_j\|_2 .
\]
The committed v40.02r07 derivation gate declares threshold $8.0$ Angstrom and minimum sequence separation $2$. A derived target contact row is therefore
\[
O_{ij}=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad d_{ij}\leq 8.0\;\mathrm{Angstrom}\ \text{and}\ j-i\geq 2.
\]
Pairs below the minimum sequence separation are retained as derived distance rows but are marked outside the current contact-map scope.

\subsection{No expanded score}

The derived coordinate/contact rows are target-only rows. They are forbidden as raw AFC/D.E.C. premises and forbidden as prediction-freeze inputs. The v40.02r06 manual-fixture contact score remains carried forward unchanged; v40.02r07 does not add PDB/mmCIF scoring, AlphaFold scoring, RMSD, TM-score, GDT, or a coordinate-level AOD prediction.

\aodnoteblock{Gate status}{This milestone exercises coordinate-payload ingestion and contact-map derivation machinery.
