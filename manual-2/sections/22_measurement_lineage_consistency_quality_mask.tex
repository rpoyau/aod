\section{Measurement-lineage consistency and quality-mask materialization}
\label{sec:pdb-measurement-consistency-quality-mask}

The target measurement lineage and the frozen AOD prediction lane are recorded
as independent branches.  They join only at a declared comparison coordinate:
\[
\begin{array}{ccl}
\text{target branch}
&:&
X_{\rm physical}
\xrightarrow{\mathcal M_\theta}Y_{\rm raw}
\xrightarrow{\mathcal Q_\psi}Y_{\rm processed}
\xrightarrow{\mathcal R_\phi}\widehat X_{\rm model}
\xrightarrow{\mathcal D_\eta}O_{\rm derived},
\\[4pt]
\text{AOD branch}
&:&
\text{AOD raw trace}
\xrightarrow{\rm freeze}X_{\rm AOD}^{\rm freeze}.
\end{array}
\]
The comparison join remains downstream of both frozen branches.

\subsection{Branch and payload-state ledgers}

The lineage ledger uses separate branch fields:
\[
\texttt{lineage\_branch},\qquad
\texttt{branch\_stage\_order},\qquad
\texttt{lineage\_object\_id},\qquad
\texttt{upstream\_object\_id}.
\]
The target branch carries stages 1--5.  The AOD prediction branch starts at its
own stage 1.  The join declaration is recorded in
\texttt{pdb\_external\_comparison\_join\_declaration.csv}.

Experimental payload state is decomposed into
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
\texttt{archive\_listing\_status}, &
\texttt{byte\_probe\_status}, &
\texttt{byte\_lock\_status},\\
\texttt{parse\_status}, &
\texttt{field\_availability\_status}. &
\end{array}
\]
For the committed coordinate model the byte payload is hash locked and parsed.
A deterministic release-local parsed snapshot of the official validation report
is also hash locked and materialized.  It is distinct from the original archive
PDF/XML/CIF byte streams, whose byte locks remain unresolved.  Reflection and
map candidates remain in their declared probe lanes.

\subsection{Quality-rule freeze}

The local-support rule is frozen before each residue/pair support materialization.  The rule
card records required fields, missing-field handling, RSRZ and RSCC policy,
occupancy and alternate-location policy, missing-density policy, observable-aware
validation-outlier policy, residue support states, pair support, and target-state
admission.  The release-local parsed snapshot remains distinct from the original
archive validation payload.

The residue support domain is
\[
\{\texttt{quality\_supported},
  \texttt{quality\_ambiguous},
  \texttt{quality\_excluded}\}.
\]
A pair receives a binary contact target only when both residues are
\texttt{quality\_supported}; all other pair states carry
\texttt{abstain}.

\subsection{Comparison-space activation}

The comparison-space matrix records parallel lanes:
\[
\text{registry},\quad
\text{coordinate model},\quad
\text{derived observable},\quad
\text{raw measurement},\quad
\text{processed measurement}.
\]
The current AOD contact-pair packet supports the derived contact/reclosure lane.
The target-side ternary mask opens when
\[
N_{\rm quality\ supported\ pairs}>0.
\]
The AOD comparison join is a separate, stricter gate:
\[
N_{\rm quality\ supported\ pairs}>0
\quad\land\quad
\text{declared alignment/projection rule}
\quad\land\quad
N_{\rm in\ scope}>0.
\]
The current quality mask has zero admitted pairs and the external alignment
coverage remains zero. Target joins, residual rows, and score audit therefore
remain closed.
