
\section{External PDB accession scope gate}

The next contact-map step is not another score. It is an accession gate: the external PDB target is named, the boundary policy is fixed, and the coordinate payload remains deferred until a later ingest gate. This keeps the AOD packet upstream of the target lane.

\subsection{Declared accession packet}

Declare the external target locator
\[
  \mathrm{source}=\mathrm{RCSB\ PDB},\qquad
  \mathrm{accession}=\texttt{1CRN},\qquad
  \mathrm{chain}=A.
\]
The gate records the already committed locator rows \texttt{pdb\_1crn\_A\_locator} and \texttt{pdb\_1crn\_A\_mmcif\_target}. The coordinate payload is not committed in this gate:
\[
  \mathrm{coordinate\_payload\_status}=\texttt{deferred\_not\_ingested}.
\]
A payload hash is required before any later accession-derived contact score:
\[
  \mathrm{coordinate\_payload\_sha256}=\texttt{required\_before\_score}.
\]

\subsection{Boundary lock}

The contact boundary is declared before payload ingest or scoring:
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \mathrm{atom\ selector}&=C\alpha,\\
  d_{\rm cut}&=8.0\,\text{\AA},\\
  \min |i-j|&=3.
\end{aligned}
\]
Evaluation pairs are intentionally not enumerated here. They must be declared after a coordinate payload is ingested and before any residual score is computed. Negative-support rows are also mandatory before classifier metrics such as MCC are read.

\subsection{Freeze-before-target order}

The valid order for a later external-accession pilot is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{scope row}
&\rightarrow \text{frozen AOD contact packet}\\
&\rightarrow \text{external target row}\\
&\rightarrow \Delta_Z,\delta_3\\
&\rightarrow \text{score audit}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The detection packet remains
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{ChainWordSpec + ReadOnlyTrace}
&\rightarrow \text{AOD motif / curling-curls specification}\\
&\rightarrow \text{SADAR context}\\
&\rightarrow \text{frozen contact packet}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The external accession never enters the raw D.E.C. row, the trace, the AOD motif packet, or the SADAR freeze.

\subsection{Ledger pointers}

The accession scope ledgers are:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{scope} & \texttt{pdb\_external\_accession\_scope\_declaration.csv},\\
\text{provenance} & \texttt{pdb\_external\_accession\_target\_provenance.csv},\\
\text{boundary} & \texttt{pdb\_external\_accession\_boundary\_lock.csv},\\
\text{leakage} & \texttt{pdb\_external\_accession\_leakage\_checks.csv}.
\end{array}
\]
The manifest records this as a scope gate only: no contact score, no coordinate-level metric, and no released folding value map.
