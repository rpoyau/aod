\section{PDB contact-comparison scope declaration gate}

The v40.02r08 milestone declares the comparison scope for a future expanded AOD-versus-PDB contact audit. It is not itself a score. It reads the already frozen v40.02r05 AOD contact/reclosure packets and the v40.02r07 target-only PDBx/mmCIF-derived contact rows only to declare the admissible future comparison envelope.

The gate exists to prevent an accidental transition from target derivation to scoring. Before any expanded AOD-versus-PDB row may be emitted, the package must declare the score scope, pair policy, leakage checks, residual coordinates, and target provenance.

\subsection{v40.02r08 files}

The v40.02r08 files are:

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Declared future comparison envelope}

For the committed manual seed fixture, the future comparison envelope is the pair subset with minimum sequence separation $2$ and contact threshold $8.0$ Angstrom. The scope gate identifies the already frozen AOD pair $1$--$3$ and the derived PDBx/mmCIF target pair $1$--$3$ as admissible to a later comparison gate, but it does not compute a confusion row in this milestone.

The allowed future contact metrics are limited to pair-level contact metrics: TP, FP, FN, TN, precision, recall, $F_1$, Jaccard, MCC, and the contact residual coordinate $\Delta Z_{\mathrm{contact}}$ with its retained $\delta_3$ class. RMSD, TM-score, GDT, and coordinate-level structure scores remain forbidden unless a coordinate-level AOD prediction freeze is declared in a later milestone.

\subsection{Leakage rule}

PDBx/mmCIF coordinate payloads, derived distance rows, and derived contact rows remain target-only rows. They are forbidden as raw AFC/D.E.C. premises and forbidden as v40.02r05 prediction-freeze premises. A later score gate may read them only after the frozen AOD prediction packets remain immutable and the declared v40.02r08 scope gate passes.

\subsection{No score in this milestone}

The v40.02r06 manual-fixture score rows are carried forward unchanged. The v40.02r07 derived PDBx/mmCIF contact rows are carried forward unchanged. The v40.02r08 package declares comparison scope only; it does not add an expanded AOD-versus-PDB score, AlphaFold score, RMSD, TM-score, GDT, coordinate-level AOD prediction, released $\lambda_{\mathrm{fold}}$, or biological-function claim.
