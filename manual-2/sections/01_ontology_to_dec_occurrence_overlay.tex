
\section{Ontology-to-D.E.C. occurrence overlay}

This Manual-II overlay records motif-family and scoped-occurrence cards without changing the raw D.E.C. row form. The row form remains the finite AFC edge-execution ledger introduced above. The ontology card names what family is being followed; D.E.C. records the execution; A$\Omega$D names the detected motif after the trace has been written; SADAR records the returned-current context; target lanes join downstream.

\[
\begin{array}{c}
\text{motif-family registry}\to\text{scoped occurrence card}\to\text{raw D.E.C. rows}\\[0.25em]
\to\text{read-only trace}\to\text{A$\Omega$D motif / curling-curls detection}\\[0.25em]
\to\text{SADAR context}\to\text{frozen packet}\to\text{downstream target map}.
\end{array}
\]

The scoped occurrence is a dataset card, not a new raw-row ontology. For a motif family
\[
M_{\rm cc}\in\mathcal M_{\rm curl},
\]
Manual II records an occurrence card as
\[
\mathcal O=(a,M_{\rm cc},B,\omega,\lambda_{\rm scale}),
\qquad
 a\vdash M_{\rm cc}(B,\omega,\lambda_{\rm scale}).
\]
Here $a$ is the declared fractal coordinate, $B$ is the boundary/window package, and $\lambda_{\rm scale}$ names the Manual-II scale lane. This card points to the raw execution ledger; it does not replace the D.E.C. columns.

The raw D.E.C. row therefore stays in the shared form
\[
(B,v,e,v_e,\sigma(e),\operatorname{adm}(e;B),w(e;B),P(e;B),\operatorname{route}(e;B)),
\]
with any occurrence identifier stored only in the Manual-II registry and manifest ledgers. The valid detection chain is trace-first:
\[
\texttt{trace\_mol\_005}
\rightarrow
\texttt{motif\_mol\_005}
\rightarrow
\texttt{sadar\_mol\_005}
\rightarrow
\texttt{chain\_GAS\_tripeptide\_seed}.
\]

They are Manual-II bookkeeping ledgers; no scientific row, target row, residual row, or score row is recomputed here.
