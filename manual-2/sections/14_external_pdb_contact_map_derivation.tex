\section{External PDB contact-map derivation gate}

This gate derives target-contact rows from the locked external PDB residue-coordinate table and then stops.  It is a target-side contact-map construction gate, not an AOD prediction join and not an external residual score.

\paragraph{Declared contact policy.}
The residue table is the chain-A, model-1, C$\alpha$ table for 1CRN.  The contact policy is declared before pair generation:
\[
  |\mathrm{label\_seq}_j-\mathrm{label\_seq}_i|\geq 3,
  \qquad
  d_{ij}^{C\alpha}\leq 8.0\,\text{\AA}.
\]
The first inequality is the sequence-separation gate.  It is part of the target-contact definition, not a score-time option.

\paragraph{Pair count.}
The chain contains 46 selected C$\alpha$ rows.  Therefore the unordered pair count is
\[
  \binom{46}{2}=1035.
\]
The declared sequence-separation rule excludes the 45 adjacent pairs and the 44 next-nearest pairs:
\[
  1035-45-44=946.
\]
Thus the target contact map has 946 eligible rows.  Applying the 8.0 \AA{} C$\alpha$ cutoff gives
\[
  N_{\mathrm{contact}}=114,
  \qquad
  N_{\mathrm{noncontact}}=832.
\]

\paragraph{Ledger order.}
The derivation reads only the residue-coordinate table and its SHA-256 manifest.  It emits a distance ledger and an eligible contact-map ledger:
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
\texttt{pdb\_external\_residue\_coordinate\_table.csv} & \longrightarrow &
\texttt{pdb\_external\_contact\_distance\_matrix.csv},\\
\texttt{pdb\_external\_contact\_distance\_matrix.csv} & \longrightarrow &
\texttt{pdb\_external\_contact\_map\_derived.csv}.
\end{array}
\]
The external target rows remain downstream objects.  The future target join still occurs after the AOD motif / curling-curls specification and SADAR context have already frozen the prediction packet.

\paragraph{Current gate status.}
This gate records the target contact map.  Evaluation-pair boundary selection, AOD-vs-target residual rows, score audit, RMSD, TM-score, GDT, AlphaFold comparison, coordinate-level AOD prediction, and any released folding value map belong to later declared gates.
