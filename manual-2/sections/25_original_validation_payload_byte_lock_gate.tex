\section{Original validation-payload byte-lock gate}

This gate locks the original wwPDB validation archive payloads used by the 1CRN target lineage. The locked archive objects are distinct from the release-local parsed validation snapshot. The comparison lane remains closed: this gate records bytes, regenerates the snapshot, and audits field equivalence.

\subsection{Inventory-driven bundle embedding}

The external-payload inventory is the authoritative embedding allowlist. Every inline bundle asset has one source path, one stable bundle path, one origin class, one byte count, and one SHA-256. Unregistered files are not embedded, and every required inventory row must resolve and verify before bundle construction.

The origin classes are
\[
\texttt{archive\_external},
\qquad
\texttt{release\_local\_derived},
\qquad
\texttt{release\_policy\_metadata}.
\]

Small validation and coordinate payloads are embedded inline. Large raw-image collections use a separate versioned payload pack or a manifest-only external lock according to the frozen embedding policy.

\subsection{Gate status}

This gate admits archive-byte provenance and snapshot-equivalence rows only. The frozen AOD packet remains upstream of every target map. Target joins, residual rows, score audits, coordinate metrics, AlphaFold comparison, coordinate-level AOD prediction, and an active $\lambda_{\rm fold}$ remain at later declared gates.
