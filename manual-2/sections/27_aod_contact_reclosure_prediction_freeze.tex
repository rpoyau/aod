\section{AOD contact/reclosure prediction freeze}

The v40.02r05 milestone freezes AOD contact/reclosure prediction packets before any protein target comparison is read. This is the first Manual II protein-facing prediction packet, but it is deliberately narrow: it emits contact/reclosure motifs only. It is not a full three-dimensional structure predictor and it is not a folding score.

The freeze preserves the ordering:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}X@{}}
\texttt{ChainWordSpec} \textrightarrow{} \texttt{Raw D.E.C. trace} \textrightarrow{} \texttt{DetectedChainMotif} \\
\texttt{SADAR context} \textrightarrow{} \texttt{frozen AOD contact/reclosure packet}.
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
Target rows prepared in the previous gate remain downstream and unread by the prediction-freeze generator.

\subsection{Prediction-freeze files}

The v40.02r05 files are:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
File & Role \\
\midrule
\texttt{aod\_folding\_prediction\_model\_registry.csv} & model contract, allowed inputs, forbidden input classes, and freeze status \\
\texttt{aod\_contact\_prediction\_freeze.csv} & frozen contact-pair predictions from detected chain motifs \\
\texttt{aod\_reclosure\_motif\_predictions.csv} & frozen reclosure motif packets and predicted pair spans \\
\texttt{aod\_prediction\_freeze\_manifest.json} & prediction-freeze manifest, leakage policy, and next scoring gate \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

The prediction rows read only the committed molecular chain-spec, read-only trace, detected-motif, and SADAR-context tables. They do not read PDB/mmCIF rows, AlphaFold rows, target contact-map rows, target distance-matrix rows, secondary-structure labels, confidence fields, or coordinate payloads.

\subsection{Frozen contact/reclosure packet}

A frozen contact prediction row records
\[
(i,j,\chi_{ij},s,\mu,T,M,S),
\]
where $(i,j)$ is the residue-pair address, $\chi_{ij}$ is the predicted contact flag, $s$ is the pair separation, and $(\mu,T,M,S)$ identify the model, trace, detected motif, and SADAR-context rows used to freeze the packet. These identifiers point to internal AOD/D.E.C. fixtures only.

The companion reclosure row records the predicted reclosure span and its predicted pair list. For example, the tripeptide seed carries adjacent route-support pairs and a multi-link reclosure span. These rows are frozen for later comparison; they are not scores.

\aodnoteblock{Freeze rule}{AOD contact/reclosure packets are frozen before any PDB/mmCIF, AlphaFold, contact-map, distance-matrix, secondary-structure, or confidence row is read. Target rows can only be used by a later residual/comparison gate.}
