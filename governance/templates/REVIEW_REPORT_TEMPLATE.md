# Review Report [anchor]

## Setup
Bind the fixed candidate payload digest, delta digest, governance digest, reviewer class, and review scope.

## Governing constraints
Cite exact AF, AFC, cycle-policy, active-goal, and style-profile rule IDs.

## Checks
Record independent materialization, hash, test, render, schema, and counterfactual checks.

## Findings
For each finding, state severity, blocking status, affected distinction/relation, evidence, and required action.

## Counterfactuals
State the malformed or adversarial packets tested and their observed structured outcomes.

## Falsification
State conditions under which the review verdict would no longer hold.

## Provenance
List exact bundle paths, hashes, and internal anchors.

## Verdict
Use one of: FEEDBACK_REQUIRED, GO_RECOMMENDED, HOLD.
