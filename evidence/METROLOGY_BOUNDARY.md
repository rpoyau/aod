Native counts and calibrated reports

Closure phases, atomic traversals, recurrence counts, RD support, RCD and its declared duration accessor are exact native records. No echo count, equality or Fourier display yields a measured time, frequency, energy, length or empirical prediction by itself.

A report x=N*n requires its declared measurand, protocol, reference, coupling, detector, calibration, unit, resolution, uncertainty, repeatability and traceability. The fixture N=3,n=2/3 gives x=2; N=4 gives 8/3 and rejects the unjustified 3*n substitution. These are exact calibration arithmetic examples, not new measured data. Existing Manual measured/application records keep their own acquisition and external-comparison scopes.
