\section{Scope and calculation policy}

Manual II carries the fusion-scale worked rows as a standalone manual. It uses the shared D.E.C. record discipline, table discipline, exact-arithmetic policy, and external-comparison quarantine. AFC runs raw; the trace records the run; A$\Omega$D names detected structure.

\subsection{Run sheet FFS-0}

Every worked row in Manual II instantiates the same finite run sheet:
\[
S_{\mathrm{FFS}}=(\zeta,\ell,R_{\zeta},\partial R_{\zeta},\omega,F_K,\mathsf D,\pi).
\]
The entries mean:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{0.96\linewidth}{@{}p{0.20\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule
symbol & operational role \\
\midrule
$\zeta$ & declared coordinate or octave in which the row is read \\
$\ell$ & declared level of the finite run \\
$R_{\zeta}$ & finite region of vertices used by the row \\
$\partial R_{\zeta}$ & declared boundary edges available to the row \\
$\omega$ & finite evaluation window \\
$F_K$ & retained extraction: which admitted boundary weights enter the exact packet \\
$\mathsf D$ & detector or SADAR-context rule applied to the trace \\
$\pi$ & target/report map, applied only after the exact row is frozen \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\subsection{Raw D.E.C. row schema}

A raw D.E.C. row carries finite boundary execution. Its worksheet columns follow the CSV-facing ledger schema:
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{0.98\linewidth}{@{}llllllllll@{}}
\toprule
\texttt{tick} & \texttt{B} & \texttt{v} & \texttt{e} & \texttt{v\_e} & \texttt{sigma\_e} & \texttt{adm\_e\_B} & \texttt{w\_e\_B} & \texttt{P\_e\_B} & \texttt{route\_e\_B} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
The row records the finite boundary execution. Registry labels, PubChem, RDKit, UniProt, PDB/mmCIF, AlphaFold, contact maps, scores, and residuals attach downstream after freeze. D.E.C. is the manual ledger format for finite AFC edge execution.

\subsection{Operational row contract}

Each worked row follows the same execution order:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.3em]
  \item declare $S_{\mathrm{FFS}}=(\zeta,\ell,R_{\zeta},\partial R_{\zeta},\omega,F_K,\mathsf D,\pi)$;
  \item fill the raw D.E.C. worksheet rows;
  \item form the retained trace $\Trace$ from $F_K$;
  \item apply the detector or SADAR context $\mathsf D(\Trace)$;
  \item freeze the exact packet $X_{\mathrm{AOD}}$;
  \item apply the target map $\pi(X_{\mathrm{AOD}})$ only after the freeze;
  \item compute the residual or $\delta_3$ audit;
  \item point to the CSV ledger that records the row.
\end{enumerate}

The CSV is the ledger. The manual example is the construction of the row that appears in the ledger.

\aodnoteblock{Scope lock}{Manual II is an operational extension of the original manual. It carries the same calculus through a declared octave and observable dictionary after exact rows are frozen.}

\subsection{Column convention}

\]
Named columns are used instead of bare positional vectors whenever an arithmetic row is shown.

\section{Scope}\label{manual:scope}
This manual carries application and prediction-test material downstream from the main A\(\Omega\)D note. The main note carries the AF/AFC/A\(\Omega\)D calculus. This manual carries calculation records, setup diagrams, comparison targets, external-comparison records, error audits, and audit tables.

\subsection{Use of this manual}\label{manual:scope:use}
The manual uses the same section / subsection / subsubsection structure as the main note. Rest-energy calculation records are organized under \secref{manual:rest-energy-prediction}. External comparison material remains downstream and quarantined from the A\(\Omega\)-internal scoped-flux calculation.


\subsection{Simulation reading route}\label{manual:simulation-reading-route}
For a compact release reading order, use the following route: main note \S\S1--3 for the AFC seed, Null/cut, branch/hinge/branch, and Q4/tesseract support; main note \S\S4--5.8 for curl, duon current, boundary, RD/RCD, pressure, and SADAR; main note \S\S5.13--5.14 for Fractal Field Ring Gate and field dynamics; manual \S\S1--2 for the calculation spine and D.E.C. ledger; manual \S\S8.1--8.3 for data-support classes and scoped errors; manual \S\S8.6--8.7 for the SPARC G0 radial shell scoring lane; manual App.~C for the 3D coordinate phase-cycle fixture; and manual \S\S9--10 for lensing data gates, registry records, and completion tables.





\subsection{Calculation spine}\label{manual:calculation-spine}
The manual uses one active calculation language. A calculation declares
\[
B=(\partial_TF,\omega).
\]
It forms retained cycle provenance, evaluates branch/hinge/branch, and computes a SADAR value:
\[
C_f[\Sigma]_{\AO},
\qquad
\mathsf{AO}_{\mu}(C)=\langle\Pi_\alpha,H_\mu,\Pi_\Omega\rangle,
\qquad
\SADARop_B(C)=p^D_{C,\AO}A_{C,\AO}.
\]
The boundary value is stored by
\[
\Phi^D_{\partial_T R}=\sum_{e\in\partial_T R}\SADARop_B(C_e).
\]
Tables store evaluated values. Tables are not the calculus.

\subsection{Worked-calculation release}\label{manual:worked-calculation-release}
A manual worked record carries a complete row: setup, declared A\(\Omega\) calculation, comparator or target, residual/error coordinate, and audit.

For an exact A\(\Omega\)-internal fixture, form
\[
\text{setup}
\to
A\leftrightarrow_{\mu}\Omega\text{ calculation}
\to
\text{fixture comparator}
\to
\Delta^{\mathbb Z},\delta_3
\to
\text{audit}.
\]

For an external comparison calculation, form
\[
\text{setup}
\to
A\leftrightarrow_{\mu}\Omega\text{ calculation}
\to
\text{observable / target record}
\to
\Delta,\epsilon,z,\sigma
\to
\text{error audit}.
\]
The symbols \(\Delta\), \(\epsilon\), \(z\), and \(\sigma\) belong to the external comparison layer. A \(z\)-score is formed only when target uncertainty is declared.

For a benchmark-dataset calculation, record
\[
\mathbf a\vdash(F,R,\partial_T R,\omega),\quad
\mathcal D_{\mathrm{bench}},\quad
\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{required}},\quad
\mathcal S=\text{benchmark manifest}.
\]
Registry rows carry benchmark calculations until the declared dataset, required coordinate, report map, and audit fields are supplied.

\aodnoteblock{Placement rule}{A worked record carries a complete simulation or comparison row. Registry rows carry declared setup, target, comparator, residual, uncertainty, and audit fields as they are supplied.}
\aodnoteblock{Specification discipline}{A rest or prediction record may use a compact curling-curl specification only after \(HT(K;B)\) is declared or inherited.}

\subsection{Table discipline}\label{manual:table-discipline}
Tables in this manual store calculated values, target values, residuals, and audit records. Manual rows, ledgers, tables, records, registries, and fixtures are document containers. They record declared values. The calculation is the declared boundary computation
\[
\mathbf a\vdash(F,R,\partial_T R,\omega),
\qquad
B=(\partial_TF,\omega),
\]
followed by the A\(\Omega\) and SADAR calculation on that boundary.


\subsection{Provenance-label policy}\label{manual:provenance-label-policy}
Tokens of the form \texttt{main:<anchor>} and \texttt{manual:<anchor>} are cross-document provenance anchors. They refer to the corresponding source label \texttt{<anchor>} in the named document. Explicit alias labels declare alternate anchors. Provenance records remain internal-reference lists; external sources belong in Literature note records.

\subsection{Exact arithmetic and presentation decimals}\label{manual:exact-arithmetic-presentation}
A\(\Omega\)-internal calculations use integer counts and exact rational ratios. Decimal values appear only as external comparison coordinates, presentation values, or plot labels.
\[
\text{A\(\Omega\) side: integers / fractions},
\qquad
\text{external side: value }\pm\text{ declared uncertainty}.
\]
If an external measured decimal is used as a comparison target, the comparison record declares its uncertainty \(\sigma\) when a \(z\)-score is formed. Percent errors may be shown as presentation comparisons when the target value is declared.

\aodnoteblock{\(\delta_3\) exact-fixture package}{For an exact internal integer fixture, define
\[
\Delta_k^{\mathbb Z}=T_k^\times-O_k,
\qquad
s_k^{(3)}=\operatorname{sgn}(\Delta_k^{\mathbb Z}),
\qquad
m_k=|\Delta_k^{\mathbb Z}|,
\qquad
\delta_{3,k}(T^\times,O)=(s_k^{(3)},m_k).
\]
Then
\[
\delta_3(T^\times,O)=\left(\delta_{3,k}(T^\times,O)\right)_{k\in K}.
\]
The symbol \(\delta_3\) records the exact ternary signed residual for an internal integer fixture.}

\aodnoteblock{Decimal precision record}{A released decimal presentation value carries
\[
(x_{\mathrm{disp}},u_x,\mathrm{unit}(x),\mathrm{src}(x),\mathrm{round}(x),\mathrm{scope}(x)).
\]
Here \(\mathrm{scope}(x)\in\{\text{internal},\text{external},\text{measured},\text{derived external},\text{value map},\text{demonstration}\}\). The precision field \(u_x\) records declared display uncertainty.}


\subsection{External-comparison quarantine}\label{manual:external-comparison-quarantine}
External constants, measured-sector units, and external comparison symbols are quarantined. They can compare against A\(\Omega\) outputs in a comparison record, but they do not enter the A\(\Omega\) calculation and do not set A\(\Omega\) notation. In this manual, an external comparison is written
\[
\Qext(\nu_{x,\AO};\mathcal T_x),
\]
where \(\mathcal T_x\) is a declared measured-sector target record.


\subsection{Scoped errors by data-support class}\label{manual:scoped-errors-data-support}
A simulation or comparison is classified by the declared data support it instantiates. Dataset resolution selects the data-support class: radial rows instantiate G0; radial rows plus proxy density/current fields instantiate G1; three-dimensional density/velocity/current fields instantiate G2; time-ordered tracer positions/velocities instantiate G3. Each class reports residuals in its declared coordinate. Projection and marginalization maps carry their own comparison coordinates and uncertainty records.

The scoped-error chain is
\[
D\rightarrow R(D)\rightarrow \mathcal M(D,R)
\rightarrow \Pi_{\mathrm{report}}
\rightarrow \epsilon_{\mathrm{report}}.
\]
Here \(D\) is the declared data-support distinction layer, \(R(D)\) is the induced relation family, \(\mathcal M(D,R)\) is the AFC/A\(\Omega\)D model evaluated on that cut, \(\Pi_{\mathrm{report}}\) is the report or projection map, and \(\epsilon_{\mathrm{report}}\) is the residual relative to the declared output target. Every manual comparison declares its data-support class before residuals are interpreted.

\paragraph{Simulation data card.}
Each simulation or comparison row records
\[
(D,\ C_{\mathrm{support}},\ M_{\AO},\ \Pi_{\mathrm{report}},\sigma_{\mathrm{input}},\ \epsilon_{\mathrm{report}},\ \mathrm{src}).
\]
Here \(D\) is the declared input data, \(C_{\mathrm{support}}\) is the data-support class, \(M_{\AO}\) is the AFC/A\(\Omega\)D calculation or map, \(\Pi_{\mathrm{report}}\) is the reported coordinate, \(\sigma_{\mathrm{input}}\) is the declared input uncertainty when present, \(\epsilon_{\mathrm{report}}\) is the residual/error coordinate, and \(\mathrm{src}\) records the data source or source artifact.
