\section{Exact Native D.E.C., Causal Duration, and Metric-Projection Protocol}
\label{app:shared-native-exactness-protocol}

\subsection{Purpose and layer separation}

This appendix fixes one cross-manual protocol for exact AFC/D.E.C. execution, native causal-duration packets, exact downstream report maps, independently frozen observation packets, and typed residuals.  It separates four questions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item Was the native calculation declared and executed correctly?
  \item Was the downstream projection declared and executed correctly?
  \item Is the target supported by its measurement lineage, uncertainty packet, and comparison scope?
  \item Does the supported prediction agree with the supported target?
\end{enumerate}

The common spine is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{curling-curl family specification}
\longrightarrow
\text{scoped occurrence}
\longrightarrow
\text{D.E.C. execution}
\longrightarrow
\text{read-only trace}
\\[-0.2em]
&\longrightarrow
\text{A$\Omega$D motif / curling-curls detection}
\longrightarrow
\text{ADAR/SADAR}
\longrightarrow
\text{native exact packet}
\\[-0.2em]
&\longrightarrow
\text{optional exact report projection}
\longrightarrow
\text{independent observation join}
\longrightarrow
\text{typed residual}.
\end{aligned}}
\]

The pre-run motif-family card and the post-trace detected motif are distinct.  The specification states what recurring family is being sought; the read-only trace supplies the evidence from which A$\Omega$D names the detected occurrence.  SADAR promotion follows returned-current detection.  External report or target maps remain downstream of the frozen native packet.

The resulting type boundary is
\[
\boxed{
\text{native exactness}
\neq
\text{projection injectivity}
\neq
\text{observation certainty}
\neq
\text{empirical agreement}.
}
\]

\subsection{Curling-curl family and scoped occurrence}

Let
\[
M_{\mathrm{cc}}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{curl}}
\]
be a recurring curling-curl motif-family specification.  A scoped occurrence card is
\[
\mathcal O
=
(a,M_{\mathrm{cc}},B,\omega,\lambda_{\mathrm{oct}}),
\qquad
a\vdash M_{\mathrm{cc}}(B,\omega,\lambda_{\mathrm{oct}}).
\]
Here $a$ is the declared fractal coordinate, $B$ is the boundary/window package, $\omega$ is the finite execution window, and $\lambda_{\mathrm{oct}}$ identifies the octave/application lane.  The occurrence card is bookkeeping context; it does not add fields to the raw D.E.C. row.

For a local tesseract witness,
\[
c=(a,\epsilon,\mu),
\qquad
\epsilon\in Q_4=\{0,1\}^4,
\qquad
\mu\in\{-1,0,+1\}.
\]
The D.E.C. ledger records execution of the occurrence at tick $t$:
\[
\mathrm{D.E.C.}_e(\mathcal O,t)
=
(t,B,c,e,v_e,\sigma_e,\operatorname{adm}_t(e;c,B),w_t(e;c,B),P_t(e\mid c,B),\operatorname{route}_t(e;c,B)).
\]

\subsection{One exact D.E.C. kernel family}

Every finite kernel row uses the same normalization law:
\[
\boxed{
P_t(e\mid c,B)
=
\frac{\operatorname{adm}_t(e;c,B)\,w_t(e;c,B)}
{\displaystyle\sum_f\operatorname{adm}_t(f;c,B)\,w_t(f;c,B)}
}
\]
with
\[
\operatorname{adm}_t(e;c,B)\in\{0,1\},
\qquad
w_t(e;c,B)\in\mathbb Q_{>0}.
\]
Define the exact normalizer
\[
Z_t(c;B)=\sum_f\operatorname{adm}_t(f;c,B)w_t(f;c,B).
\]
When $Z_t(c;B)>0$, the probabilities are exact rationals and satisfy
\[
\sum_e P_t(e\mid c,B)=1
\]
by exact arithmetic.

\paragraph{Isotropic specialization.}
If $w_t(e;c,B)=1$ for every admissible edge, then
\[
P_t^{\mathrm{iso}}(e\mid c,B)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal A_t(c;B)|}.
\]
For a full unblocked $Q_4$ cell,
\[
P^{\mathrm{iso}}=(1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4).
\]

\paragraph{Anisotropic specialization.}
With declared exact weights
\[
w=(1,1,4,2),
\qquad
Z=8,
\]
the same kernel gives
\[
P^{\mathrm{ani}}=(1/8,1/8,1/2,1/4).
\]
Equal anisotropic weights reduce exactly to the isotropic specialization; no second normalization law is introduced.

\paragraph{Blocked hinge and slide pool.}
A blocked route has
\[
\operatorname{adm}(e_0;B)=0.
\]
The same kernel removes it before normalization.  For the declared slide weights
\[
w'=(3,3,1),
\qquad
Z'=7,
\]
one obtains
\[
P_{\mathrm{slide}}=(3/7,3/7,1/7).
\]

\paragraph{Terminal state.}
When $Z_t(c;B)=0$, no division is performed.  The row records the declared terminal policy state:
\[
\texttt{terminal},
\qquad
\texttt{blocked},
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
\texttt{pending}.
\]

\subsection{Execution modes and read-only trace}

The kernel is distinct from its execution mode.  Every run freezes one of:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.1em,style=nextline]
  \item[\texttt{enumerate\_all}] propagate every admissible successor with exact rational branch mass;
  \item[\texttt{deterministic\_selector}] choose one edge under a frozen selector rule;
  \item[\texttt{sample\_one}] choose one edge under a frozen random algorithm and seed.
\end{description}

The execution ledger records at least
\[
\begin{gathered}
\texttt{kernel\_id},\quad
\texttt{kernel\_sha256},\quad
\texttt{execution\_mode},\quad
\texttt{selector\_id},\\
\texttt{rng\_algorithm},\quad
\texttt{rng\_seed},\quad
\texttt{selected\_edge},\\
\texttt{incoming\_mass\_num/den},\quad
\texttt{outgoing\_mass\_num/den}.
\end{gathered}
\]
A probability table is therefore never confused with the realized or propagated trace.  The read-only trace is hash-frozen before A$\Omega$D motif detection, returned-current pairing, ADAR promotion, or SADAR evaluation.

\subsection{Pen-and-paper D.E.C. audit}

A hand audit follows this exact order:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item declare the curling-curl motif-family specification $M_{\mathrm{cc}}$;
  \item declare the scoped occurrence $\mathcal O$;
  \item declare the local cell $c=(a,\epsilon,\mu)$;
  \item list all Hamming-1 successor edges;
  \item write the admissibility mask $\operatorname{adm}(e_i;B)$;
  \item write the exact weights $w(e_i;B)$;
  \item calculate $Z$ and every exact $P_i$, then verify $\sum_iP_i=1$ when $Z>0$;
  \item declare the execution mode and record the realized or propagated row;
  \item freeze the read-only trace;
  \item identify declared returned-current row pairs;
  \item promote the detected pair to ADAR;
  \item compute SADAR and the boundary audit.
\end{enumerate}

The hand ledger and software ledger must agree exactly.  A disagreement is an $E_{\mathrm{calc}}$ defect; it is not repaired by changing a target or report map.

\subsection{ADAR/SADAR promotion}

After the returned-current relation is detected, write
\[
\operatorname{ADAR}_e(B)
=
(A_e(B),D_e(B),R_e^{\mathrm{asym}}(B)).
\]
The declared returned-current duration and window clip give
\[
\rho^D_{\omega,e}
=
\min\bigl(\operatorname{dur}(RCD_e),|\omega|_e\bigr),
\qquad
p^D_e=C_{3,e}\rho^D_{\omega,e}.
\]
The SADAR contribution is
\[
\phi_e=p^D_eA_e,
\qquad
A_F(B)=\sum_e\phi_e.
\]
A kernel-weighted expected SADAR readout is a separate row:
\[
A_F^{\mathrm{exp}}(B\mid t)
=
\sum_e P_e(B\mid t)p^D_e(B)A_e(B).
\]
Thus the three roles are recorded separately:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
$P_e$ & D.E.C. kernel weight,\\
$\mathrm{pD}_e A_e$ & SADAR contribution,\\
$P_e\,\mathrm{pD}_e A_e$ & kernel-weighted expected SADAR.
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Here $\mathrm{pD}_e$ is the serialized ledger label for the exact quantity $p_e^D$.

\subsection{Exact metric-projection contract}

A report map
\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{met}}:\mathfrak X_{\mathrm{native}}\longrightarrow\widehat X_{\mathrm{model}}
\]
is exact when its domain, codomain, basis, origin, orientation, scale, alignment, clipping rule, denominator policy, missing-value policy, and serialization profile are frozen before target values enter.

For rational coordinates
\[
\widehat{\mathbf x}_i
=
\left(\frac{a_i}{d},\frac{b_i}{d},\frac{c_i}{d}\right),
\qquad a_i,b_i,c_i,d\in\mathbb Z,\quad d>0,
\]
the squared distance is exact.  For a rational threshold $T=p/q$, the comparison is performed without square roots or floats by integer cross multiplication:
\[
q^2\bigl[(a_i-a_j)^2+(b_i-b_j)^2+(c_i-c_j)^2\bigr]\le p^2d^2.

Both sides are integers.  Exactness does not imply injectivity.  Projection information loss is recorded separately as
\[
\mathcal I_{\Pi}
=
(\text{injectivity status},\text{discarded distinction},\text{inverse-domain status}).
\]
Declared non-injectivity is never entered as $E_{\mathrm{proj}}$.  The latter is reserved for projection-contract, basis, denominator, alignment, serialization, or deterministic execution defects.

\subsection{Octave refinement and projection information loss}

A lawful exact change under a frozen octave-refinement relation is recorded as $\Delta_{\mathrm{oct}}$. Without a refinement map, common codomain, comparison relation, and pass/fail condition, both fields remain blocked.

An exact projection may collapse distinct native packets.  Such collapse is recorded in $\mathcal I_{\Pi}$ and never silently treated as arithmetic error.  Round-trip checks are required only when an inverse or partial inverse is explicitly declared on a stated domain.

\subsection{Application-lane implications}

\paragraph{Elementary lane.}
Native retained-capacity and closure rows remain distinct from registry, isotope-mass, spectrum, lifetime, ionization, stellar, and SI-time report maps.  The caesium-133 elementary occurrence card is not the caesium SI reference card.

\paragraph{Molecular lane.}
Exact formula and CHNOPS closure rows remain native.  Time, rate, spectrum, geometry, mass, and thermochemical quantities enter through separately frozen maps.  In particular,
\[
X_{\mathrm{shedding}}\xrightarrow{\Pi_{\mathrm{thermo}}}\Delta H_{\mathrm{report}}
\]
is a report-map contract, not an identity.

\paragraph{Tau, Higgs, field, solar, and SPARC lanes.}
Native candidate, support, returned-current, pressure, and field rows remain separate from lifetime, mass, unit, benchmark, uncertainty, and score maps.  A changed target support or report map does not by itself change the native packet.

\paragraph{Protein/PDB lane.}
Coordinate-derived contact bits, measurement-supported target states, A$\Omega$D contact packets, and empirical score rows remain separate.  A coordinate fixture may retain binary contact bits while the quality-supported target mask abstains.  That is a target-support reclassification, not a native prediction change.
